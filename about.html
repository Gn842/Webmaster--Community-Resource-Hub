About Lee County


Lee County was founded in 1887 and became a Charter County upon approval of the voters on Nov. 5, 1996, effective Jan. 1, 1997. The County encompasses 1,212 square miles and is home to various birds, wildlife, and ecosystems that make the area perfect for work and play.


Lee County Manager & Departments


Lee County Government consists of 20 departments under the office of the County Manager. These departments function to maintain the various facets of public services. To learn more about County departments, their services, and locations that all work together to make Lee County such a special place browse our interactive map, Explore Lee, and other informational tools online - Community Engagement Tools.


History of Lee County, Florida


Lee County has a long, rich history spanning over 130 years in Southwest Florida. Below is a timeline of major events since our founding in 1887:


May 13, 1887

Lee County is founded.
After gaining independence from Monroe County, Lee County is created by act of legislature.

December 1915

Lee County Courthouse opens.

1924

Tamiami Trail Bridge is constructed.
Construction of the bridge across the river spurs growth in Fort Myers.

1925

Spring training comes to Lee County
Connie Mack and the Philadelphia Athletics begin spring training at Terry Park.

April 26, 1928

Tamiami Trail is opened.
At a cost of $8.0 million or $25,000 per mile, Tamiami Trail is opened.

1955

Lehigh Acres is acquired.
Lee Ratner acquires 18,000 acres of land that would become Lehigh Acres.

May 26, 1963

Sanibel Causeway is opened.
Sanibel Causeway opens, connecting Sanibel to the mainland of Lee County.

1996

Conservation 20/20 program is established.
The Conservation 20/20 program is created through voter referendum with the goal of conserving 20,000 acres of natural habitat by 2020.

August 1997

FGCU holds its first classes.
After selecting the site in 1992, FGCU holds its first classes in fall 1997.

January 1, 1997

Lee County becomes a charter county.
Upon voter approval in 1996, Lee County became a charter county.
